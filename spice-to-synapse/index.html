Three AI Tools

Pick a Tool. Start Now.

All three tools live inside one file. Open it once and every module works — online or offline, on any device.

MODULE 01
🔬
Plant → Drug Screener

Type any African medicinal plant or isolated compound. Get a complete drug-potential report — binding strength, safety profile, disease targets, and a plain-English summary — in under one second.

  • Predicted binding against AChE, BACE1, NF-κB, NRF2 and 4 more neuro targets
  • Full drug-likeness check — can it become a pill?
  • Blood-brain barrier penetrance prediction
  • Disease pathway map — Alzheimer's, stroke, or both
  • Lead grade (A / B / C) with collaboration recommendation

MODULE 02
🧬
Virtual Preclinical Cohort

Generate a complete virtual animal study before running a single real experiment. Predict biomarker responses, statistical power, and expected AI classification accuracy — saving months and reducing animal use.

  • Predicted biomarker trajectories across all treatment groups
  • Statistical power analysis and recommended sample size
  • Reduces required real animals by up to 60%
  • NC3R-compliant ethical benefit statement
  • Expected ML accuracy benchmarked against validated data
Open Virtual Cohort →
MODULE 03
🤖
Human Trial Simulator

The bridge between animal data and the first human dose. Predict safe starting dose, Phase I adverse events, Phase II efficacy, IND readiness, and NAFDAC regulatory pathway — before any patient is enrolled.

  • Allometric dose scaling — rat to human (FDA method)
  • Phase I safety simulation with dose escalation
  • Phase II efficacy prediction and sample size
  • West African population pharmacogenomics included
  • IND readiness score (0–100) + NAFDAC pathway
