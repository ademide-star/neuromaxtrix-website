Who We Are

About ANHRL

"To map, model, and mitigate neurological disease risk in Africa by combining biological research, data analytics, and innovative digital solutions."

🌍 Our Vision

To become a leading African hub for neurohealth innovation, driving globally relevant research that improves brain health outcomes and informs policy, education, and clinical practice.

💡 Our Approach
At ANHRL, we believe the future of neuroscience lies in integration:
biology + computation + visualization
We do not just study the brain — we model it, map it, and translate it into solutions that matter.
🧬
Translational Neuroscience

Investigating molecular and cellular mechanisms underlying neurodegenerative and cerebrovascular diseases — inflammation, oxidative stress, and neurotransmitter dynamics.

🧠
Stroke & Dementia Research

Developing population-specific insights into stroke risk, cognitive decline, and neurodegeneration — with a focus on early detection and prevention in African populations.

💻
Computational Neuroscience

Building predictive models and simulations to understand brain function and disease progression, integrating biomarkers such as cytokines and neural signaling pathways.

📊
Scientific Visualization

Designing high-quality research graphics, dashboards, and AI-driven tools that translate complex data into actionable insights for clinicians, researchers, and policymakers.
