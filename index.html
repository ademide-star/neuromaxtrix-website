About NeuroMatrix BioSystems

Transforming biology through intelligence, innovation, and impact through interdisciplinary research, computational science, biomedical innovation, and educational technology.

Who We Are

NeuroMatrix BioSystems is an interdisciplinary research and innovation platform dedicated to advancing biomedical science through the integration of neuroscience, translational bioinformatics, computational modeling, artificial intelligence, and scientific visualization.

We bridge the gap between wet laboratory research and dry laboratory analytics, transforming biological discoveries into predictive models, digital tools, educational technologies, and evidence-based healthcare solutions.

Our work spans neurological disorders, cardiovascular diseases, precision medicine, computational biology, and AI-driven decision support, with a strong commitment to improving health outcomes in Africa and beyond.

NeuroMatrix BioSystems serves as the parent organization for specialized research units, including the African NeuroHealth Research Lab (ANHRL), fostering multidisciplinary collaboration among researchers, clinicians, educators, data scientists, and innovators.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about NeuroTrack Pro licensing, academic usage, pricing, delivery, and support.

What is included in the license? ▼
Your license unlocks full access to the NeuroTrack Pro web app and desktop Windows .exe installer. It covers Morris Water Maze, Y-Maze, and Open Field Test analysis including video upload, auto-tracking, trajectory, heatmap, metrics, PNG export, and CSV export.
How do I receive my license key? ▼
After payment via Paystack or bank transfer, your unique license key is emailed to you within 24 hours. For Paystack payments, delivery can be automated within minutes.
Is this a one-time payment or subscription? ▼
It is a one-time payment with perpetual access. Researcher and Institution plans can include upgrade support depending on the license level.
Can I use this for my PhD thesis? ▼
Yes. NeuroTrack Pro is designed for academic research and can be cited in your methods section where appropriate.
How does NeuroTrack Pro compare to ANY-maze? ▼
NeuroTrack Pro is designed as a lower-cost, accessible alternative for institutions that need flexible behavioral analysis without high hardware costs, making it especially suitable for research environments with tighter budgets.
What if I need a refund? ▼
We offer a 7-day refund policy if the software does not work as described. Contact neuromatrixbiosystems@gmail.com with your license key and a short explanation.
