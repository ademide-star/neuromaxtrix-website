About the Digital Poster Exhibition

A proposed digital alternative to traditional poster boards.

Why Digital Posters?

Instead of relying entirely on physical poster boards, researchers can present their work through an online conference exhibition.


Each presenter receives a dedicated poster page containing their research poster, five-minute presentation, abstract, and a live question section.

Possible Conference Features

  • Digital poster gallery
  • Five-minute presentation videos
  • Interactive poster pages
  • Questions to presenters
  • QR codes for individual posters
  • People's Choice voting
  • Research categories
  • Digital conference archive

Future Development

This prototype can later be connected to a database so presenters can upload their own posters and videos.


It can also include presenter accounts, administrator approval, live Q&A, voting, QR codes and automatic generation of individual poster links.
